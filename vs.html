Solar System
Sun
The Sun is the star at the center of the Solar System. It is a massive ball of hydrogen and helium that provides light and heat to all the planets through nuclear fusion.
Moon
Earth's only natural satellite, the Moon orbits our planet and influences tides. Its surface is covered in craters, mountains, and vast plains called maria.
Earth
Our home planet, Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet with life. It has a diverse environment with oceans, continents, and a protective atmosphere.
Mercury
The smallest and innermost planet in the Solar System, Mercury is a rocky world with extreme temperature variations, ranging from -180°C to 430°C.
Venus
Often called Earth's sister planet, Venus is similar in size but has a thick atmosphere of carbon dioxide that creates a runaway greenhouse effect, making it the hottest planet.
Mars
The Red Planet, Mars has a thin atmosphere and surface features including the largest volcano in the Solar System, Olympus Mons, and the vast Valles Marineris canyon system.
Jupiter
The largest planet in our Solar System, Jupiter is a gas giant with a Great Red Spot storm that has been raging for centuries. It has 79 known moons, including the four Galilean moons.
Saturn
Famous for its spectacular ring system, Saturn is another gas giant. Its rings are made of ice particles, rock, and dust, creating one of the most beautiful sights in the Solar System.
Uranus
The seventh planet from the Sun, Uranus is unique because it rotates on its side. It's an ice giant with a blue-green color due to methane in its atmosphere.
Neptune
The farthest planet from the Sun, Neptune is another ice giant with the strongest winds in the Solar System, reaching speeds of up to 2,100 km/h.
